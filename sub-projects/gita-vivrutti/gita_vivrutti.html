Śrī Rāghavendra Tīrtha (1623–1671 A.D.)

Ācārya · Tattvavāda Vedānta

Śrī Rāghavendra Tīrtha is one of the most prolific writers of the Tattvavāda system of Vedānta. Apart from being a great mystic, he was a very powerful exponent of Tattvavāda Vedānta. His works range from the Vedic literature to the stotra literature. He has commented upon seventeen Ṭīkā works of Śrī Jayatīrtha. For his contribution, he enjoys the title 'Ṭippanyācāryacakravartī'.

His another great contribution is the interpretation of the original texts — Brahmasūtras, Bhagavadgītā, and Upaniṣads — on the lines of the commentaries of Śrī Madhvācārya. For this particular contribution he stands almost unequalled among Tattvavāda writers. He is also known for his simple and lucid style, tremendous clarity and astonishing simplicity.

His present work, the Gītā Vivṛtti (also known as Gītārthasaṅgraha), is a direct commentary on Śrī Bhagavadgītā. It is of immense help to students and scholars alike in understanding the Tattvavāda interpretation of the Gītā. He has also written glosses on the commentaries Gītābhāṣya (Prameyadīpikā) and Gītātātparya (Nyāyadīpikā) of Śrī Jayatīrtha.
