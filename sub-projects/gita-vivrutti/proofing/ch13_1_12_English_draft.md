## 13.1

*अर्जुन उवाच*

- **प्रकृतिं² पुरुषं चैव क्षेत्रं क्षेत्रज्ञमेव च ।**  
  Prakṛti and Puruṣa, and likewise the kṣetra and the kṣetrajña —
- **एतद्वेदितुमिच्छामि ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं च केशव ॥१॥**  
  this I wish to know, along with knowledge and the object of knowledge, O Keśava. ॥ 1 ॥

**1.s1** — पूर्वषट्कद्वयोक्तार्थसङ्ग्रहपरोऽयमध्यायः ।  
→ This chapter is a compendium of the meanings stated in the two preceding sets of six chapters.

**1.s2** — तथाहि ।  
→ To elaborate:

⚑ **1.s3** — यत्प्रथमषट्के 'त्रैगुण्यविषया वेदाः'⁽३⁾ इत्यादिना प्राचुर्येण ज्ञानसाधनमाषष्ठसमाप्तेरुक्तम्, यच्च तत्र द्वितीयेऽध्याये 'न त्वेवाहम्'⁽४⁾ इत्यादिनाऽनादिनित्यत्वादिना जीवस्वरूपमुक्तम्, यच्च द्वितीयषट्के भगवत्स्वरूपमुक्तम्, यदपि सप्तमे 'भूमिरापः'⁽५⁾ इत्यादिना क्षेत्रशब्दितं भगवदावासस्थानमुक्तम्, तद्विकीर्णतयोक्तं सर्वं बुद्ध्यारोहार्थं सङ्क्षिप्यास्मिन्नध्याये प्रश्नपूर्वकं प्रदर्श्यते - **प्रकृतिमित्यादिना** ।  
→ In the first six chapters, the means of knowledge (jñāna-sādhana) was stated at length up to the end of the sixth chapter, beginning with “the Vedas have the three guṇas as their domain” (Gītā 2.46)³ and so on; and there, in the second chapter, with “never indeed was I not” (Gītā 2.12)⁴ and so on, the nature of the jīva was stated through its being beginningless, eternal, and so on; and in the second six chapters the nature of the Lord was stated; and also, in the seventh [chapter], with “earth, water…” (Gītā 7.4)⁵ and so on, the dwelling-place of the Lord, termed “kṣetra,” was stated. All of that, stated in scattered fashion, is condensed — to make it easy for the intellect to take in — and shown in this chapter by way of a question: “prakṛtim” etc.

   *(⚑ why: long; Gītā 7.4 'bhūmir āpaḥ' rendered as the Lord's abode termed kṣetra (as the Vivṛtti says), not as the eight-fold prakṛti.)*

**1.s4** — **पुरुषं** जीवम् ।  
→ “Puruṣa” is the jīva.

**1.s5** — **ज्ञानं** ज्ञानसाधनम् ॥१॥  
→ “Jñāna” is the means of knowledge. ॥ 1 ॥

**Footnote 2** (on प्रकृतिं) — अयं श्लोकः क्वचिन्न पठ्यते । परन्तु श्रीमदाचार्यसम्मत इति ज्ञायते ।  
→ This verse is not read in some [texts]; but it is known to be accepted by Śrīmad Ācārya (Madhva).

→ Gītā (2.46).

**Footnote 4** (on 'न त्वेवाहम्') — गी. (२-१२)  
→ Gītā (2.12).

**Footnote 5** (on 'भूमिरापः') — गी.(७-४)  
→ Gītā (7.4).


## 13.2

*श्रीभगवानुवाच*

- **इदं शरीरं कौन्तेय क्षेत्रमित्यभिधीयते ।**  
  This body, O Kaunteya, is called the “kṣetra”;
- **एतद्यो वेत्ति तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञ⁶ इति तद्विदः ॥२॥**  
  one who knows this, the knowers of it call “kṣetrajña.” ॥ 2 ॥

**2.s1** — ननु भगवत्स्वरूपज्ञानस्यैव पूर्वोक्तदिशा मुक्तिहेतुत्वात् तत्स्वरूपोक्तिर्युक्ता ।  
→ Objection: since only knowledge of the Lord’s own nature is the cause of liberation, as was said before, stating that nature [alone] would be proper.

⚑ **2.s2** — क्षेत्रस्वरूपं 'महाभूतानि'⁽२⁾ इत्यादिना किमर्थमत्रोच्यत इति चेन्न ।  
→ “Why, then, is the nature of the kṣetra stated here, with ‘mahābhūtāni’ etc.²?” — if it be so asked: no [there is no fault].

   *(⚑ why: objection-form; 'if so asked: no' – kept the Vivṛtti's own objection/answer movement.)*

**2.s3** — क्षेत्रज्ञत्वविशिष्टभगवज्ज्ञानोपयोगित्वात्, क्षेत्रोक्तिरिति क्षेत्रोद्देशपूर्वं भगवतः क्षेत्रज्ञत्वमाह - **इदमिति** ।  
→ Because it is of use for knowledge of the Lord as qualified by being the kṣetrajña, the mention of the kṣetra is [made]; so, first indicating the kṣetra, He states the Lord’s kṣetrajña-hood: “idam” etc.

**2.s4** — **कौन्तेय** ।  
→ “O Kaunteya.”

**2.s5** — **इदं शरीरम्** अव्यक्तमहदहङ्कारादिकम् ।  
→ “This body” — the unmanifest, the mahat, ahaṅkāra and the rest.

**2.s6** — भगवदावासस्थानत्वात् क्षीयते स्थीयतेऽत्र भगवतेति व्युत्पत्त्या **क्षेत्रमित्यभिधीयते** ।  
→ Because it is the Lord’s dwelling-place, by the derivation “kṣīyate — it is dwelt in here by the Lord (sthīyate ’tra bhagavatā),” it is called (abhidhīyate) “kṣetra.”

**2.s7** — **एतत्** अव्यक्तादिकं क्षेत्रशब्दितम् ।  
→ “This” — [the body] beginning with the unmanifest, termed “kṣetra.”

**2.s8** — **यो वेत्ति** जानाति ।  
→ “Who knows (vetti)” — [i.e.] understands.

**2.s9** — **तम्** ।  
→ “Him.”

**2.s10** — **तद्विदः** क्षेत्रविदः ।  
→ “Tadvidaḥ” — those who know the kṣetra.

**2.s11** — **क्षेत्रज्ञमिति प्राहु**रित्यर्थः ।  
→ “They call [him] ‘kṣetrajña’” — that is the meaning.

**2.s12** — शरीरशब्दस्याव्यक्तादिपरत्वं च स्वपरिणामदेहादिवियोगद्वारा जीवस्य हिंसादिहेतुत्वात् ।  
→ And the reason the word “śarīra” denotes the unmanifest etc. is that [it] is a cause of harm and the like to the jīva by way of separation from its own modifications, the body and so on.

**2.s13** — 'शर् शृ हिंसायाम्' इति धातोः भगवता प्रेर्यत्वादीरं च ।  
→ [It comes] from the root “śar śṛ” in the sense of harming — “śar”; and, because it is to be impelled by the Lord, “īra” as well.

**2.s14** — 'ईर् प्रेरणे' इति धातोः ।  
→ From the root “īr” in the sense of impelling.

**2.s15** — शर् च तदीरं चेति शरीरमिति व्युत्पत्तेरिति ज्ञेयम् ।  
→ “Śar” and that “īra” — thus “śarīra” by derivation — so it should be understood. (Bhāva: “śarīra” = śar, that which brings harm to the jīva + īra, that which is impelled by the Lord.)

⚑ **2.s16** — तेन 'सङ्घातश्चेतना' इति सङ्घातपदेन क्षेत्रविकारेष्वेकदेशत्वेनोक्तशरीरस्य कथं समस्तक्षेत्रत्वमुच्यत इति शङ्का निरस्ता ॥२॥  
→ Thereby is set aside the doubt: given “saṅghātaś cetanā” [13.7], how can the body, stated by the word “saṅghāta” as only one part among the modifications of the kṣetra, be said to be the whole kṣetra? ॥ 2 ॥

   *(⚑ why: 'saṅghāta' as one part vs. whole kṣetra – please check the logic of the doubt.)*

**Footnote 6** (on क्षेत्रज्ञ) — स्वात्माधारः स्वेच्छयैव जीवदृष्टेस्तिरोहितः । क्षेत्रज्ञेत्युच्यते विष्णुर्जीवस्य पुरुषोत्तमः ॥ इति च । - भा.ता. (५/११/१४)  
→ “The Lord Viṣṇu, the support of Himself, who by His own will is hidden from the jīva’s sight, is called ‘kṣetrajña’ — the Supreme Person of the jīva.” — so too in Bhāgavata-tātparya (5/11/14).

**Footnote 2** (on 'महाभूतानि') — गी. (१३-६)  
→ Gītā (13.6).


## 13.3

- **क्षेत्रज्ञं चापि मां विद्धि सर्वक्षेत्रेषु भारत ।**  
  Know Me, too, as the kṣetrajña in all kṣetras, O Bhārata;
- **क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्ज्ञानं यत् तज्ज्ञानं मतं मम ॥३॥**  
  that which is knowledge of kṣetra and kṣetrajña — that, in My view, is [true] knowledge. ॥ 3 ॥

**3.s1** — जीवस्यापि किञ्चित् क्षेत्रज्ञानात् स किं क्षेत्रज्ञः, इत्यत आह - **क्षेत्रज्ञं चेति** ।  
→ “Since the jīva too has some knowledge of the kṣetra, is he then the kṣetrajña?” — to this He says: “kṣetrajñaṃ ca” etc.

**3.s2** — **सर्वक्षेत्रेषु** अव्यक्तादिषु ।  
→ “In all kṣetras” — in the unmanifest and the rest.

**3.s3** — **क्षेत्रज्ञं** तु **मां विद्धि** ।  
→ “But know Me as the kṣetrajña.”

**3.s4** — न जीवम् ।  
→ Not the jīva.

**3.s5** — क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्यज्ज्ञानं **तज्ज्ञानम्** ।  
→ The knowledge of kṣetra and kṣetrajña — that is “[the] knowledge.”

**3.s6** — **मम** सम्यग्ज्ञानं **मतमि**त्यर्थः ।  
→ “Mama samyagjñānaṃ mataṃ” — “it is considered by Me to be right knowledge” — that is the meaning.

⚑ **3.s7** — अत्र 'सर्वक्षेत्रेषु' इत्युक्तिरिदं शरीरमित्यत्र शरीरपदोक्ताव्यक्तादेः क्षीयतेऽत्रेश्वरेणेति व्युत्पत्त्या क्षेत्रपदवाच्यत्वमिति सूचनाय ॥३॥  
→ Here, the expression “in all kṣetras” is for the purpose of indicating that the unmanifest etc., denoted by the word “śarīra” in “idaṃ śarīram,” are called by the word “kṣetra” through the derivation “kṣīyate ’tra īśvareṇa” (it is dwelt in here by the Lord). ॥ 3 ॥

   *(⚑ why: uses your corrected 'kṣīyate ’tra īśvareṇa'; I render 'dwelt in here by the Lord' (kṣi = to dwell).)*


## 13.4

- **तत्क्षेत्रं यच्च यादृक् च यद्विकारि यतश्च यत् ।**  
  That kṣetra — what it is, of what kind it is, what modifications it has, and from what [source] it arises —
- **स च यो यत्प्रभावश्च तत्समासेन मे श्रुणु ॥४॥**  
  and who he is [that knows it], and what his power is — hear that from Me in brief. ॥ 4 ॥

**4.s1** — क्षेत्रादिस्वरूपं वक्तुं प्रतिजानीते - **तत्क्षेत्रमिति** ।  
→ Wishing to state the nature of the kṣetra and the rest, He announces: “tat kṣetram” etc.

**4.s2** — **तत्क्षेत्रं** यद्भगवन्निवासस्थानतया क्षेत्रपदोक्तम् ।  
→ “That kṣetra” — that which, as the Lord’s dwelling-place, was spoken of by the word “kṣetra.”

⚑ **4.s3** — देहद्वारा जीवहिंसाहेतुतया ईश्वरप्रेर्यत्वहेतुना च शरीरपदेन प्रकृतम् अव्यक्तादि **यच्च** यत्स्वरूपं **यादृक्** यत्प्रकारं **यद्विकारि** ।  
→ That unmanifest etc. which, through the body, as a cause of harm to the jīva and as that which is impelled by the Lord, was introduced by the word “śarīra” — “yac ca,” of what nature it is; “yādṛk,” of what kind; “yadvikāri,” with what modification.

   *(⚑ why: the compressed reason for the word 'śarīra' (harm to the jīva + impelled by the Lord) – please check the syntax of my rendering.)*

**4.s4** — कर्मधारयात् मतुप् ।  
→ [The word is formed by adding] the suffix matup to a karmadhāraya [compound].

**4.s5** — येन विकारेण युक्तम् ।  
→ [It means] endowed with which modification.

**4.s6** — **यतः** यादृशप्रवृत्त्यनुकूलधर्मयुक्तात् पुरुषात् ।  
→ “Yataḥ” — from the person endowed with the properties conducive to whatever kind of activity.

**4.s7** — **यत्** इणः शत्रन्तं रूपम् ।  
→ “Yat” — the present-participle (śatṛ) form of the root “iṇ.”

**4.s8** — गतिमत् ।  
→ “Gatimat” — moving.

**4.s9** — प्रवर्तत इति यावत् ।  
→ That is, “acting.”

**4.s10** — **स च** प्रवर्तकः ।  
→ “Sa ca” — that impeller as well.

**4.s11** — **यः** यत्स्वरूपः ।  
→ “Yaḥ” — of what nature.

⚑ **4.s12** — **यत्प्रभावः** यादृशप्रवृत्त्यनुकूलधर्मेतरसामर्थ्यवान् ।  
→ “Yatprabhāvaḥ” — possessed of a capacity other than the properties conducive to whatever kind of activity.

   *(⚑ why: 'dharmetara-sāmarthya' – rendered literally; sense is uncertain.)*

**4.s13** — **तत्** सर्वम् ।  
→ “All that.”

**4.s14** — **समासेन** सङ्क्षेपेण ।  
→ “Samāsena” — in brief.

**4.s15** — **मे** सकाशात् ।  
→ “From Me.”

**4.s16** — **श्रुणु** श्रुत्वा निष्ठां कुरु ॥४॥  
→ “Hear” — having heard, make it your firm conviction. ॥ 4 ॥


## 13.5

- **ऋषिभिर्बहुधा गीतं ¹छन्दोभिर्विविधैः पृथक् ।**  
  Sung by the sages in many ways, separately in various meters,

→ “Brahma-sūtras are the Śārīraka-sūtras” — Gītā-bhāṣya. “Brahma-sūtras are the statements that point to Brahman” (Prameya-dīpikā). “Brahma [means] the Veda, its meaning, or the Supreme Brahman; the sūtras of that are the Brahma-sūtras — that is, the sūtras meant to determine the nature of the Supreme Brahman, Viṣṇu, who embodies the meaning of the entire Veda.” — Nyāya-sudhā. “Hence the Śārīra is the Supreme Self alone.    *(⚑ why: 'atiśayena vikāritayā' – rendered literally.)*

**Footnote 1** (on इच्छा) — आनन्दानुभवस्तूम उत्कृष्टानुभवात्स्मृतः । तद्युक्तत्वं यथा सौम्यं गुणानामधिकं हि तत् । भक्तयादिगुणपूगोऽपि दुःखहेतुत्वभावनात् । निष्फलो भवति ह्यद्धा प्रीतस्य सफलो भवेत् । तस्मादानन्दमानं तु गुणेषूत्कृष्टमुच्यते । तस्याभिमानी ब्रह्मैको भक्तिज्ञानादिकस्य च । श्रद्धाभिमानिनी देवी तथैव तु सरस्वती । तदन्येषां गुणानां तु तदनु ये विबुधाः स्मृताः । गुणानां तु प्रभुर्ब्रह्मा तस्मादेकश्चतुर्मुखः । औत्पत्तिकगुणो नाम शुभप्राप्त्यैकयोग्यता । तस्याभिमानी प्राणस्तु स हि सर्वगुणाधिक इति च । गुणिनां मध्ये गुणिनि स्थितमौत्पत्तिकगुणरूपं सूत्रमित्यर्थः । गुणिनां गुणयोग्यत्वं यत्सर्वगुणिषु स्थितम् । वायुस्तदभिमान्येकः सर्वगुण्यधिकस्ततः ॥ इति प्रभञ्जने ।  
→ “The experience of bliss is held to be the best of all experiences. To be endowed with it — as with mildness (saumya) — is indeed superior among the guṇas. [The reason:] even a whole host of qualities such as devotion becomes fruitless when it is thought of as a cause of pain, whereas for one who is delighted it bears fruit. Therefore the state of bliss alone is declared the best among the guṇas. Its presiding deity is Brahmā alone — [he presides] also over devotion, knowledge and the like; likewise the goddess who presides over faith (śraddhā) is Sarasvatī. The meaning is this: the innate guṇa abides in each possessor of guṇas, like a thread (sūtra) running through them. That fitness for guṇas which abides in all possessors of guṇas — Vāyu alone is its presiding deity; therefore he excels all possessors of guṇas.” — so in the Prabhañjana.


## 13.8

- **अमानित्वमदम्भित्वमहिंसा क्षान्तिरार्जवम् ।**

## 13.11

## 13.12

- **अध्यात्मज्ञाननित्यत्वं तत्त्वज्ञानार्थदर्शनम् ।**  
  constancy in knowledge concerning the Supreme Self, and vision of the object of true knowledge —
- **एतज्ज्ञानमिति प्रोक्तमज्ञानं यदतोऽन्यथा ॥१२॥**  
  this is declared to be [the means of] knowledge; whatever is otherwise than this is ignorance. ॥ 12 ॥

**12.s1** — **अध्यात्मज्ञाननित्यत्वम्** परमात्मानमधिकृत्य स्थिते ज्ञाने नियतत्वम् ।  
→ “Adhyātmajñānanityatvam” — steadiness in knowledge established with reference to the Supreme Self.

**12.s2** — **तत्त्वज्ञानार्थदर्शनम्** ।  
→ “Tattvajñānārthadarśanam” —

**12.s3** — अपरोक्षज्ञानार्थं शास्त्रपर्यालोचनम् ।  
→ Reflection on the scriptures for the sake of direct knowledge.

**12.s4** — **एतत्** 'अमानित्वम्' इत्यादिनोक्तं विंशतिधर्मजातम् ।  
→ “This” — the group of twenty dharmas stated by “amānitvam” etc.

⚑ **12.s5** — **ज्ञानमिति** ज्ञायतेऽनेनेति करणव्युत्पत्त्या ज्ञानसाधनं प्रोक्तम् ।  
→ “Jñānam iti” — by the derivation “jñāyate ’nena” (that by which one knows), [the group] is said to be the means of knowledge.

   *(⚑ why: karaṇa-derivation 'jñāyate ’nena' (as accepted) – check that 'the group' is the right subject.)*

**12.s6** — **अतोऽन्यथा यत्** अस्ति **तदज्ञानं** ज्ञानसाधनं नेत्यर्थः ।  
→ “Ato ’nyathā yat asti tad ajñānam” — whatever exists otherwise than this is ignorance; it is not a means of knowledge — that is the meaning.

⚑ **12.s7** — यद्वा - ज्ञानशब्दः भावव्युत्पत्त्या संवित्परश्च ।  
→ Or: the word “jñāna,” by the derivation in the sense of the action itself (bhāva), denotes awareness (saṃvit) as well.

   *(⚑ why: bhāva-vyutpatti sense.)*

**12.s8** — तत्त्वज्ञानार्थस्य तत्त्वज्ञानविषयस्य विष्णोर्दर्शनम्, अपरोक्षज्ञानमित्यर्थः ।  
→ “Tattvajñānārthasya” — of the object of true knowledge — “darśanam” — the vision of Viṣṇu; that is, direct knowledge (aparokṣa-jñāna).

**12.s9** — एतद्भगवद्विषयापरोक्षज्ञानं ज्ञानमिति प्रोक्तम् ।  
→ This direct knowledge of the Lord is declared to be “jñāna.”

**12.s10** — **अतोऽन्यथा यत् तज्ज्ञानं** न भवतीति ।  
→ “Ato ’nyathā yat” — whatever is otherwise than this is not knowledge.

**12.s11** — इह ज्ञानशब्दो ज्ञानतत्साधनोभयपरः ।  
→ Here the word “jñāna” denotes both knowledge and its means.

**12.s12** — तत्त्वज्ञानार्थदर्शनमित्ययमप्युक्तदिशा शास्त्रपर्यालोचनरूपज्ञानसाधनपरः, अपरोक्षज्ञानपरश्च ।  
→ And “tattvajñānārthadarśanam” too, as explained, denotes the means of knowledge consisting in scriptural reflection, and also direct knowledge.

⚑ **12.s13** — ततश्चैतच्छब्देनोभयं परामृश्य ज्ञानशब्देन भावकरणोभयव्युत्पत्त्या ज्ञानसाधनत्वज्ञानत्वयोर्विधानं युक्तमिति ज्ञेयम् ॥१२॥  
→ Therefore, referring to both by the word “etat,” the predication by the word “jñāna” of being a means of knowledge and of being knowledge — through the derivation in both the bhāva and the karaṇa senses — is proper: so it should be understood. ॥ 12 ॥

   *(⚑ why: concluding resolution of 12.s5/12.s7.)*
