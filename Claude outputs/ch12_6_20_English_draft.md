# Gītā Vivṛtti — Chapter XII, 12.6–12.20 — English draft for review

## 12.6

- **ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।**  
  But those who, having renounced all actions in Me, hold Me as their supreme [goal],
- **अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥६॥**  
  worship Me, meditating on Me with unswerving yoga alone — ॥ 6 ॥

**6.s1** — मदुपासने न तथा क्लेश इत्याह - **ये त्विति** ।  
→ He says that in worshipping Me there is no such difficulty: "ye tu" etc.

**6.s2** — पूर्वस्माद्वैलक्षण्यद्योतक**स्तु**शब्दः ।  
→ The word "tu" signals a distinction from the preceding [case].

**6.s3** — **ये तु **।  
→ "Ye tu" — those who, however —

**6.s4** — **सर्वाणि कर्माणि** ।  
→ "All actions" (sarvāṇi karmāṇi).

**6.s5** — **मयि** ।  
→ "In Me" (mayi).

⚑ **6.s6** — **सन्न्यस्य** सर्वस्वतन्त्रो हरिरेव मयि स्थित्वा स्वपूजात्वेन कर्माणि स्वाधीनकर्त्रा मया कारयतीत्यनुसन्धाय समर्प्येति वा ।  
→ "Having renounced" (sannyasya) — having kept in mind that Hari alone, the independent Lord of all, abiding in me, has actions performed through me, the dependent agent, as His own worship; or [the sense is] "having offered" (samarpya).

**6.s7** — **मत्पराः** अहमेवोत्तम इति जानन्तः ।  
→ "Matparāḥ" — knowing "I alone am the highest."

**6.s8** — **अनन्येनैव योगेन** अव्यक्तोपासनां विना कृतेन ध्यानेन केवलमद्विषयध्यानेनापीति यावत् ।  
→ "With unswerving yoga alone" (ananyena eva yogena) — with meditation performed without worship of the avyakta; that is, with meditation on Me alone.

**6.s9** — **मां ध्यायन्तः** ।  
→ "Meditating on Me" (māṃ dhyāyantaḥ).

**6.s10** — **उपासते** ।  
→ "They worship" (upāsate).

**6.s11** — अत्र उपासत इत्युक्त्या पर्युपासत इत्युक्ताव्यक्तोपासनमिव नातिशयितोपासनम् ।  
→ Here, by saying "upāsate" [rather than "paryupāsate"], it is shown that this is not an exceptionally intense worship, like the worship of the unmanifest spoken of with "paryupāsate".

**6.s12** — अतीवेन्द्रियनियमनादिकं मत्प्रसादे नापेक्षितमिति न क्लेश इति सूचितम् ।  
→ Intense restraint of the senses and the like is not required for My grace; hence there is no difficulty — this is hinted.

**6.s13** — तथा, 'न चिरात्' इत्यनेनाव्यक्तापरोक्षे यावान् प्रयासस्तावता यदि मामुपासते तदूनेन वा तदा मदपरोक्षमेव भवतीत्यतोऽपि न क्लेश इति सूचितम् ।  
→ Likewise, by "na cirāt" ("before long"), it is hinted that there is no difficulty here either: whatever effort is needed for direct realization of the unmanifest — if they worship Me with that much, or even less, then direct realization of Me comes about.

⚑ **6.s14** — 'तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता भवामि' इत्यनेनेन्द्रियसंयमाद्यूनभावेऽपि देवीवद्देवो नोपेक्षते, किन्तु तानि साधनानि भक्तिमतः स्वयमेवाप्रयत्नेन ददातीति चातिसौकर्यं भक्तानां भगवदुपासने इति भावः ।  
→ By "teṣām ahaṃ samuddhartā bhavāmi" it is shown that even where sense-restraint and the like fall short, the Lord — just as the Devī [too] — does not neglect them; rather, He Himself gives those means, without effort [on their part], to the devotee. Thus the ease of the devotees' worship of the Lord is exceedingly great — this is the sense.

**6.s15** — यद्वा - 'क्लेशोऽधिकतर' इति श्लोके अव्यक्तोपास्तिमार्गस्य क्लेशतरत्वोक्त्यैव सुष्ट्वाचारादेरूनत्वेऽपि मत्प्रसादो भवत्येवेत्यक्लेशत्वात्, मदुपास्तिमार्ग एव श्रेष्ठ इति तात्पर्यगत्योक्तप्रायत्वात्, हेत्वन्तरेणापि भगवदुपास्तिमार्गः श्रेष्ठ इत्याह - **ये त्विति** ।  
→ Or [take it thus]: in the verse "kleśo 'dhikataraḥ…", by stating that the path of worship of the unmanifest is more difficult, it has been virtually stated, by implication of its purport, that My grace comes even when good conduct and the like fall short — since [My path] is free from such difficulty — and that the path of My worship alone is superior. So, by another reason also, He says that the path of worship of the Lord is superior: "ye tu" etc.

**6.s16** — पूर्वार्धस्य प्रागिवार्थः ।  
→ The first half has the same meaning as before.

⚑ **6.s17** — अनन्येनैव अन्यशब्दो लक्ष्म्याद्यन्यदेवतावाची सन् तन्निष्ठस्वातन्त्र्यपरिवारत्वविषयपरः, स न भवतीत्यन्यः, तेन योगेन, ध्यानेन ।  
→ "Ananyena eva": the word "anya", here denoting other deities such as Lakṣmī, refers to [meditation] concerned with the independence and the retinue-status resting in them; that which is not that is "ananya" — "by that yoga", "by meditation".

**6.s18** — भगवदधीनतया तत्परिवारत्वेन लक्ष्मीब्रह्मादिसहितेन ध्यानयोगेनेति यावत् ।  
→ That is: by meditation-yoga together with Lakṣmī, Brahmā and the others, as dependent on the Lord and as His retinue.

**6.s19** — 'ॐ अङ्गावबद्धास्तु न शाखासु हि प्रतिवेदम् ॐ^२^' इत्यादि सूत्रात् ।  
→ This follows from the sūtra "Oṃ aṅgāvabaddhās tu na śākhāsu hi prativedam Oṃ" and the like.

**6.s20** — **मां ध्यायन्त उपासते** ।  
→ "Meditating on Me, they worship" (māṃ dhyāyanta upāsate). ॥ 6 ॥

**Footnote २** — ब्र.सू. (३-३-५७)  
→ Brahma Sūtra (3.3.57) — the sūtra quoted above, "aṅgāvabaddhās tu na śākhāsu hi prativedam."


## 12.7

- **तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् ।**  
  For them, I become the deliverer from the ocean of death and saṃsāra
- **भवामि न चिरात् पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥७॥**  
  before long, O Pārtha — for those whose minds are absorbed in Me. ॥ 7 ॥

**7.s1** — **तेषां** मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ।  
→ "Of them" — of those whose minds are absorbed in Me.

**7.s2** — **अहम्** ।  
→ "I."

**7.s3** — **मृत्युसंसारसागरात्** मृत्युरूपसंसारसमुद्रात् ।  
→ "From the ocean of death and saṃsāra" — from the ocean of saṃsāra, which has the nature of death.

**7.s4** — **न चिरात्** शीघ्रम् ।  
→ "Before long" (na cirāt) — quickly.

**7.s5** — **समुद्धर्ता भवामी**त्यर्थः ।  
→ "I become the deliverer" (samuddhartā bhavāmi) — this is the meaning.

**7.s6** — अत्र न चिरादित्युक्त्या श्रिया सह भगवदुपासने द्वयोरपि परमप्रसादसम्भवेन अचिरान्मोक्षो भवति ।  
→ Here, by saying "na cirāt", it is shown that when the Lord is worshipped together with Śrī, since the supreme grace of both is possible, liberation comes without delay.

**7.s7** — पृथगव्यक्तोपासनपक्षे तु पूर्वं किञ्चिद्भगवदुपासनं कृत्वा बहुकालं पृथक् श्रियमुपास्य पुनः श्रिया सह भगवदुपासनं तु निरन्तरं कार्यम्, ततो मुक्तिरिति चिरेणैव भवतीत्यतोऽचिरेण मुक्तिहेतुत्वाच्च भगवदुपासनमेवाधिकमित्युक्तं भवति ।  
→ But in the view of separate worship of the unmanifest, one first performs some worship of the Lord, then for a long time worships Śrī separately, and again must continually worship the Lord together with Śrī; only then does liberation come — thus it comes only after a long time. Therefore, because worship of the Lord leads to liberation in a short time, it follows that worship of the Lord alone is superior.

**7.s8** — तदुक्तम् - 'विष्णुना सहिता ध्याता साऽपि तुष्टिं परां व्रजेत्' इत्यादि ।  
→ So it has been said: "Meditated upon together with Viṣṇu, she too attains supreme satisfaction" etc.


## 12.8

- **मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।**  
  Fix your mind on Me alone; place your intellect in Me.
- **निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥८॥**  
  Thereafter you will dwell in Me alone — there is no doubt. ॥ 8 ॥

**8.s1** — एवमुक्तदिशा भगवदुपास्तेरेवोत्तमत्वात्, तदेव कुरु ।  
→ Thus, in the manner stated, since worship of the Lord alone is supreme, do that alone.

**8.s2** — तदशक्तौ तत्सिद्ध्यर्थम् उपायान्तराणि सम्पादयेत्याह - **मयि** इत्यादि चतुष्टयेन ।  
→ If unable to do that, one should carry out other means for its attainment — so He says, in four [verses]: "mayi" etc.

**8.s3** — **मय्येव मन आधत्स्व** आधेहि ।  
→ "Fix the mind on Me alone" — place [it] there.

**8.s4** — मामुपास्वेति यावत् ।  
→ That is, worship Me.

**8.s5** — तदलाभे, **मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय** ।  
→ Failing that: "place the intellect in Me" —

**8.s6** — मद्विषयं ज्ञानं सम्पादय ।  
→ acquire knowledge concerning Me.

**8.s7** — **अत ऊर्ध्वम्**, मत्सम्पत्त्यनन्तरम् ।  
→ "Thereafter" — after the attainment of Me.

**8.s8** — **मय्येव निवसिष्यसि** ।  
→ "You will dwell in Me alone."

**8.s9** — निर्दुःखानन्दानुभवात्मिकां स्थितिं प्राप्स्यसि ।  
→ You will attain the state that consists in the experience of sorrowless bliss. ॥ 8 ॥


## 12.9

- **अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् ।**  
  If, however, you are unable to fix the mind steadily on Me,
- **अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनञ्जय ॥९॥**  
  then by the yoga of repeated practice seek to attain Me, O Dhanañjaya. ॥ 9 ॥

**9.s1** — **अथे**त्यर्थान्तरे ।  
→ "Atha" — in the sense of another alternative.

**9.s2** — **स्थिरं** यथा तथा ।  
→ "Steadily" — in such a way that it is steady.

**9.s3** — **मयि चित्तं समाधातुं न** **शक्नोषि** ।  
→ "If you cannot fix the mind on Me."

**9.s4** — **ततो** मनःसमाधानाशक्तत्वात् ।  
→ "Then" — because of the inability to fix the mind.

**9.s5** — पुनः पुनः प्रत्याहारेण भगवन्मनःसमाधानरू**पाभ्यास**लक्षणोपायेन ।  
→ By the means characterized by abhyāsa-yoga — the practice of fixing the mind on the Lord — through repeated pratyāhāra (withdrawal [of the senses]).

**9.s6** — **मामाप्तुं** ज्ञातुम् **इच्छ** ।  
→ "To attain Me" — to know [Me]; "seek." ॥ 9 ॥


## 12.10

- **अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव ।**  
  If you are unable even for practice, become one to whom My work is supreme;
- **मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन् सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि ॥१०॥**  
  doing actions even for My sake, you will attain perfection. ॥ 10 ॥

**10.s1** — **अभ्यासेऽप्यशक्तोऽसि** ।  
→ "If you are unable even for practice" (abhyāse 'py aśakto 'si).

**10.s2** — तदा **मत्कर्मपरमः** मत्प्रीत्यर्थं मद्विषयकर्मानुष्ठानमेव परममुत्तमं यस्य स तथा तादृशः ।  
→ Then "matkarmaparamaḥ": one for whom the performance of actions directed to Me, for My pleasure, is itself the highest and best — such a one.

**10.s3** — **भव** ।  
→ "Become."

**10.s4** — तावता कथमिष्टसिद्धिरित्यत आह - **मदर्थमिति** ।  
→ To the question, "How can the desired accomplishment come by that much?" He says: "madartham" etc.

**10.s5** — **कुर्वन्नपी**त्यर्थः ।  
→ "Even while doing" — this is the meaning. ॥ 10 ॥


## 12.11

- **अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः ।**  
  If you are unable even to do this, then, resorting to union with Me (My yoga),
- **सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान् ॥११॥**  
  renounce the fruit of all actions, with the self restrained. ॥ 11 ॥

**11.s1** — **अथेति** प्राग्वत् ।  
→ "Atha" — as before.

**11.s2** — एतदपि सर्वाण्यपि जपार्चनादीनि कर्माणि मदुद्देश्यकानि मत्प्रीत्यर्थकानीत्यनुष्ठानमपि **कर्तुमशक्तोऽसि** तर्हि देवतान्तरोद्देशेन कर्माणि कृत्वाऽन्ते मयि समर्पणरूपं मद्योगम् ।  
→ "Even this": if you are unable even to undertake [the thought that] all actions — worship, japa and so on — are directed to Me and are for My pleasure, then, performing actions with other deities as their aim and at the end offering them to Me — that is "My yoga", consisting of offering.

**11.s3** — मत्कर्मवत्वे उपायम् अन्ते मयि समर्पणम् ।  
→ The means to having actions that are Mine — offering [them] to Me at the end.

**11.s4** — सर्वकर्मणां वैष्णवत्वेन करणे य उपायस्तमाश्रितो भव ।  
→ Be one who has resorted to the means by which all actions are performed as belonging to Viṣṇu.

**11.s5** — **ततो **मद्योगात् ।  
→ "Then" — from that yoga of Mine.

**11.s6** — **यतात्मवान्** नियतमनस्कः ।  
→ "Yatātmavān" — with a restrained mind.

**11.s7** — **सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं** **कुर्वि**त्यर्थः ।  
→ "Renounce the fruit of all actions" — this is the meaning.

**11.s8** — सर्वप्रकारेणापि मदुपासनमेव त्वदुपलक्षितैः सर्वैः कार्यमिति तात्पर्यार्थः ।  
→ The purport is that worship of Me alone must be done, in every way, by all who are represented by you [Arjuna]. ॥ 11 ॥


## 12.12

- **श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं² विशिष्यते ।**  
  Knowledge is indeed better than practice; meditation excels knowledge;
- **ध्यानात् कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥१२॥**  
  renunciation of the fruit of action [excels] meditation; and upon renunciation, peace follows immediately. ॥ 12 ॥

**12.s1** — 'मयि' इत्यादिनोक्तध्यानालाभे ज्ञानं तदलाभेऽभ्यासयोग इत्येतत् समर्थयते - **श्रेयो हीति** ।  
→ He substantiates this: where the meditation spoken of in "mayi" etc. is not attained, there is knowledge; where that too is not attained, there is the yoga of practice — "śreyo hi" etc.

**12.s2** — अज्ञानपूर्वकादभ्यासाज्ज्ञानं **श्रेयः** ।  
→ Knowledge is better than practice that is without knowledge.

**12.s3** — **ज्ञान**मात्राज्ज्ञानपूर्वक**ध्यानं श्रेयः** ।  
→ Better than mere knowledge is meditation preceded by knowledge.

**12.s4** — तस्मा**द्ध्यानात्** ज्ञानपूर्वकसकामध्यानादित्यर्थः ।  
→ "Than meditation" — that is, than meditation that is preceded by knowledge but is desire-laden (sakāma).

**12.s5** — **कर्मफलत्यागः** श्रेयान् ।  
→ "Renunciation of the fruit of action" is better.

**12.s6** — एतादृशसकामध्यानात् कर्मफलत्यागोपलक्षिततादृशनिष्कामध्यानं श्रेय इति योज्यम् ।  
→ It is to be construed thus: better than such desire-laden meditation is desireless meditation, marked by renunciation of the fruit of action.

⚑ **12.s7** — **फलत्यागात्** कर्मफल त्यागोपलक्षितनिष्कामध्यानादित्यर्थः ।  
→ "Than renunciation" — that is, than the desireless meditation marked by renunciation of the fruit of action.

⚑ **12.s8** — अपरोक्ष**ज्ञानं** श्रेय इति योज्यम् ।  
→ It is to be construed that direct (aparokṣa) knowledge is better [than that].

**12.s9** — **अनन्तरं शान्तिः** मोक्ष इत्यर्थः ।  
→ "Immediately after, peace" — "peace" means liberation; this is the meaning. ॥ 12 ॥

**Footnote २** — 'ये भक्त्या विवशा विष्णोर्नाममात्रैकजल्पकाः । तेऽपि मुक्तिं व्रजन्त्याशु किमु तद्ध्यायिनः सदा ॥' इति व्योमसंहितायाम् । - भा.ता. (३/१०/१५)  
→ "Even those who, overpowered by devotion, merely utter the Lord's name alone, swiftly attain liberation — what then of those who always meditate on Him?" — Vyoma-saṃhitā, as cited in Bhāgavata-tātparya (3/10/15).


## 12.13

- **अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः² करुण एव च ।**  
  Hating no being, friendly and compassionate too,
- **निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥१३॥**  
  free from the sense of "mine", free from egoism, the same in pain and pleasure, forbearing, ॥ 13 ॥

**13.s1** — 'ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि' इत्युक्तम्, तदुपलक्षितधर्मान्तरोक्त्या प्रपञ्चयति - **अद्वेष्टेत्यादिना** ।  
→ What was stated in "ye tu sarvāṇi karmāṇi" He elaborates by describing further qualities implied by it: "adveṣṭā" etc.

**13.s2** — **अद्वेष्टा** अप्रियमकुर्वन् ।  
→ "Hating no one" — not doing what is disagreeable [to anyone].

**13.s3** — क्लेशस्थानं निरूप्य ततो रक्षिता **मैत्रः** ।  
→ One who, having identified the source of another's distress, then protects [him] — "friendly" (maitra).

**13.s4** — **करुणः** कृपावान् ।  
→ "Compassionate" — merciful.

**13.s5** — **निर्ममः** ममेदमिति स्वातन्त्र्यबुद्धिहीनः ।  
→ "Free from the sense of 'mine'" — devoid of the notion of independence in "this is mine."

**13.s6** — **निरहङ्कारः** अविद्यमानात्मगुणाभिमानमोहहीनः ।  
→ "Free from egoism" — devoid of the delusion of conceit about qualities that the self does not possess.

**13.s7** — हेयतया दुःखसमं वैषयिकसुखं यस्य स **समदुःखसुखः** ।  
→ One for whom worldly pleasure, regarded as something to be shunned, is equal to pain — he is "samaduḥkhasukhaḥ."

**13.s8** — **क्षमी** सहिष्णुः ।  
→ "Forbearing" — tolerant. ॥ 13 ॥

**Footnote २** — गायत्रीजपपरायणः - 'जप्येन एव तु संसिध्येत् ब्राह्मणः न अत्र संशयः । कुर्यादन्यन्न वा कुर्यात् मैत्रो ब्राह्मण उच्यते ॥' इत्युक्तेः ।  
→ ["Maitra" means] one devoted to Gāyatrī-japa — according to the statement: "By japa alone a brāhmaṇa attains perfection; there is no doubt in this. Whether he does anything else or not, the one who is friendly (maitra) is called a brāhmaṇa."


## 12.14

- **सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः ।**  
  ever content, a yogī, self-controlled, firm in conviction,
- **मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥१४॥**  
  with mind and intellect offered to Me — he who is thus My devotee is dear to Me. ॥ 14 ॥

**14.s1** — **सन्तुष्टः** अलम्बुद्धिमान् ।  
→ "Content" — one who has the sense of "enough" (alam).

**14.s2** — **योगी** कर्मध्यानरूपज्ञानोपायवान् ।  
→ "Yogī" — one who has the means to knowledge in the form of action and meditation.

**14.s3** — **यतात्मा** विषयेभ्य उपसंहृतचित्तः ।  
→ "Self-controlled" — whose mind is withdrawn from the objects of the senses.

**14.s4** — **दृढनिश्चयः** अप्रामाण्यशङ्काशून्यतत्त्वनिश्चयवान् ।  
→ "Firm in conviction" — one with a settled conviction of the truth, free from doubt as to its being unreliable (aprāmāṇya).

**14.s5** — मय्यर्पिते मनोबुद्धी यस्य स तथोक्तः ।  
→ One whose mind and intellect are offered to Me — such a one is so called. ॥ 14 ॥


## 12.15

- **यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।**  
  He from whom the world is not agitated, and who is not agitated by the world,
- **हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥१५॥**  
  who is free from elation, impatience, fear and agitation — he too is dear to Me. ॥ 15 ॥

**15.s1** — किंच, **यस्माल्लोको नोद्विजते** न बिभेति, लोकस्य भयहेतुर्यो न भवति ।  
→ Further: "by whom the world is not agitated" — the world does not fear him; he is not a cause of fear to the world.

**15.s2** — यश्च सर्वमीशाधीनं बुद्ध्वा **लोका**ज्जना**न्नोद्विजते** ।  
→ And he who, having understood that all is under the Lord's control, is not agitated by the world, by people.

**15.s3** — **यश्च** **हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैः** ।  
→ And [he who is free] from harṣa, amarṣa, fear and udvega.

⚑ **15.s4** — हर्षो नाम मदादधर्माय मनःप्रवृत्तिः अमर्षो रोषः ।  
→ "Harṣa" is the mind's inclination toward adharma out of intoxication (mada); "amarṣa" is anger.

**15.s5** — उद्वेगो मनःकम्पविशेषः ।  
→ "Udvega" is a particular trembling of the mind.

**15.s6** — तैर्मुक्तो हीनः ।  
→ "Freed" by them — devoid of them.

**15.s7** — **स चे**त्यत्र भक्त इत्यनुषज्यते ।  
→ Here, in "sa ca," the word "bhakta" is to be supplied.

**15.s8** — **मे प्रियः** ।  
→ "Dear to Me." ॥ 15 ॥


## 12.16

- **अनपेक्षः शुचिर्दक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः ।**  
  Free from expectation, pure, capable, impartial, free from distress,
- **सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥१६॥**  
  a renouncer of all undertakings — he who is thus My devotee is dear to Me. ॥ 16 ॥

**16.s1** — किंच **अनपेक्षो**^१^ भगवत्प्रीत्यन्यत्र काप्यपेक्षाहीनः ।  
→ Further: "anapekṣaḥ" — devoid of any expectation anywhere other than the Lord's pleasure.

**16.s2** — **शुचिः** बाह्यान्तरशुद्धिमान् ।  
→ "Pure" — pure outwardly and inwardly.

**16.s3** — **दक्षः** भगवत्कर्मकरणे पटुः ।  
→ "Capable" — skilled in doing the Lord's work.

⚑ **16.s4** — **उदासीनः** कर्तव्योपकारापकारयोरुपेक्षकः ।  
→ "Impartial" — indifferent in the matter of benefit and injury [as things] to be done.

**16.s5** — **गतव्यथः** परकृतापकारैरप्राप्तमनःक्लेशः ।  
→ "Free from distress" — not afflicted in mind by injuries done by others.

**16.s6** — **सर्वारम्भपरित्यागीत्यस्य** अवैष्णवसर्वारम्भपरित्यागीति सर्वारम्भाभिमानत्यागी सर्वारम्भफलत्यागी भगवति सर्वारम्भसमर्पणरूपत्यागवानिति चतुर्धार्थः ।  
→ "Renouncer of all undertakings" (sarvārambhaparityāgī) has four senses: a renouncer of all non-Vaiṣṇava undertakings; a renouncer of the conceit in all undertakings; a renouncer of the fruit of all undertakings; and one whose renunciation consists in offering all undertakings to the Lord. ॥ 16 ॥

**Footnote १** — अनपः प्राणनाथः श्रीविष्णुः, तद्विषया ईक्षा ईक्षणं दर्शनं यस्य सः तथोक्तः । - इति पदरत्नावल्याम् १/२०/१ (श्रीविजयध्वजतीर्थश्रीचरणकृतायां भागवतव्याख्यायाम्)  
→ "Anapa" is Śrī Viṣṇu, the Lord of life; one whose vision (īkṣā, darśana) is directed to Him is so called [i.e., "anapekṣa"]. — Pada-ratnāvalī 1/20/1 (Śrī Vijayadhvajatīrtha's commentary on the Bhāgavata).


## 12.17

- **यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।**  
  He who neither rejoices nor hates, neither grieves nor desires,
- **शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान् यः स मे प्रियः ॥१७॥**  
  a renouncer of the auspicious and the inauspicious, full of devotion — he is dear to Me. ॥ 17 ॥

**17.s1** — 'हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तः' इत्युक्ते निष्ठाप्रत्ययेनातीतत्वप्रतीत्या कालान्तरे हर्षादिमानिति शङ्का स्यात् ।  
→ Since "harṣāmarṣabhayodvegair muktaḥ" ("free from elation, impatience, fear and agitation") conveys, by the suffix of the past participle (niṣṭhā), a sense of [their] having ceased, a doubt might arise that at a later time he may again be subject to elation and the like.

**17.s2** — तन्निरासाय तद्व्यनक्ति **यो न हृष्यतीत्यादिना** ।  
→ To dispel that, He makes it clear: "yo na hṛṣyati" etc.

⚑ **17.s3** — 'नानद्यतनवत् क्रियाप्रबन्धसामीप्ययोः'^३^ इति सूत्रेण क्रियासातत्यरूपक्रियाप्रबन्धे विहितलट्प्रत्ययान्तत्वेन '**हृष्यति**' इत्यादेर्यावज्जीवं हृष्यतीत्यादिरर्थः ।  
→ By the sūtra "nānadyatanavat kriyāprabandhasāmīpyayoḥ", since the present-tense suffix is enjoined in the sense of continuity of the action, "hṛṣyati" etc. means "rejoices throughout life" and the like.

**17.s4** — **'न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि'** इत्यादिरूपेण नञ्सम्बन्धे सति कदाऽपि न हृष्यतीत्यादिरर्थो भवतीति भावः ।  
→ When the negation is joined, in the form "na hṛṣyati na dveṣṭi" etc., the meaning is "never rejoices" and so on — this is the idea.

⚑ **17.s5** — उदाहृतं च वृत्तौ तार्तीयतृतीयपादे ।  
→ And this has been cited in the Vṛtti, in the third pāda of the third [chapter].

**17.s6** — 'यावज्जीवमन्नमदात्' इत्यादि ।  
→ "Yāvajjīvam annam adāt" ("he gave food throughout his life") etc.

**17.s7** — 'सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी' इति सामान्यत उक्तम् ।  
→ "Sarvārambhaparityāgī" was said in general terms.

**17.s8** — विशेषोक्त्या व्यनक्ति- **शुभाशुभेति** ।  
→ He makes it specific: "śubhāśubha" etc.

**17.s9** — ज्ञानभक्तिवैराग्याद्यन्यशुभत्यागीत्यर्थः ।  
→ That is: a renouncer of auspicious things other than knowledge, devotion, dispassion and the like.

**17.s10** — अत एव तदन्यन्न काङ्क्षतीत्यर्थो न काङ्क्षतीत्यस्य ज्ञेयः ।  
→ For this very reason, "does not desire" is to be understood as "does not desire anything other than that."

**17.s11** — एवं 'लोकान्नोद्विजते' इत्यस्यापि 'हर्षामर्षभयैर्मुक्तः' इत्यनेनापौनरुक्त्यं ध्येयम् ।  
→ Likewise, it should be understood that "lokān nodvijate" is not redundant with "harṣāmarṣabhayair muktaḥ."

**17.s12** — उक्तशङ्कानिरासार्थत्वादिति ।  
→ Because it serves to dispel the doubt stated. ॥ 17 ॥

**Footnote ३** — पा. सू. ३-३-१३५  
→ Pāṇini Sūtra 3.3.135.


## 12.18

- **समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानापमानयोः ।**  
  Equal toward foe and friend, and likewise in honour and dishonour,
- **शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः ॥१८॥**  
  equal in cold and heat, in pleasure and pain, free from attachment, ॥ 18 ॥

**18.s1** — किंच 'समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च' इत्यादि ।  
→ Further: "samaḥ śatrau ca mitre ca" etc.

**18.s2** — पूर्वं 'समदुःखसुखः' इति सामान्येनोक्तम् ।  
→ Earlier, "samaduḥkhasukhaḥ" was said in general terms.

**18.s3** — तद्विशिष्योक्त्या व्यनक्ति - **शीतोष्णेत्यादि** ।  
→ He makes that specific, by stating it in particular: "śītoṣṇa" etc.

**18.s4** — शीतोष्णादिविषयजन्यसुखदुःखेषु ।  
→ In pleasures and pains born of objects such as cold and heat.

**18.s5** — **समः** उत्सेकानुत्सेकहीनः ।  
→ "Equal" — devoid of exultation and dejection.

**18.s6** — **सङ्गविवर्जितः** फलस्नेहहीनः ।  
→ "Free from attachment" — devoid of affection for the fruit. ॥ 18 ॥


## 12.19

- **तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी सन्तुष्टो येन केनचित् ।**  
  to whom blame and praise are alike, silent, content with whatever comes,
- **अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान् मे प्रियो नरः ॥१९॥**  
  homeless, of steady mind, full of devotion — that man is dear to Me. ॥ 19 ॥

**19.s1** — किंच **तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिः** ।  
→ Further: "tulyanindāstutiḥ."

⚑ **19.s2** — हेयत्वेन निन्दायास्तुल्या स्तुतिर्यस्य स तथा ।  
→ One for whom praise is equal to blame, in that [both] are to be shunned.

**19.s3** — **अनिकेतः** अनियतवासः ।  
→ "Aniketaḥ" — of no fixed residence.

**19.s4** — अकारवाच्यभगवदाश्रयो वा ।  
→ Or: one who has the Lord, denoted by the letter "a," as his refuge.

**19.s5** — **स्थिरमतिः** कुतर्कैरचञ्चलज्ञानः ।  
→ "Of steady mind" — whose knowledge is not shaken by fallacious reasoning (kutarka).

**19.s6** — 'यो मद्भक्तः', 'भक्तिमान्' इति भक्तेर्बहुवारोक्तिरद्वेष्टेत्यादिनोक्तेषु धर्मेषु भक्तेराधिक्यज्ञापनाय ।  
→ The repeated mention of devotion — "yo madbhaktaḥ," "bhaktimān" — is to show the superiority of devotion over the qualities stated in "adveṣṭā" etc.

**19.s7** — अत्र सुखादिषु साम्यं हर्षादिराहित्यं च प्रायेणेति ज्ञेयम् ।  
→ Here, equality in pleasure and the like, and freedom from elation and the like, are to be understood as "for the most part" (prāyeṇa).

**19.s8** — 'प्रायः सुखादिषु समः प्रायो हर्षादिवर्जितः' इत्यादेः ।  
→ Because of [the statement] "For the most part equal in pleasure and the like, for the most part free from elation and the like" and so on.

**19.s9** — 'सन्तुष्टः सततम्' इत्यस्य 'सन्तुष्टो येन केनचित्' इत्येतद्व्याख्यानरूपमित्यपौनरुक्त्यम् ।  
→ "Santuṣṭaḥ satatam" [14] and "santuṣṭo yena kenacit" [here] — the latter being of the nature of an explanation of the former, there is no redundancy. ॥ 19 ॥


## 12.20

- **ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते ।**  
  But those who faithfully follow this immortal-making dharma, as declared,
- **श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः ॥२०॥**  
  endowed with faith, holding Me as supreme — those devotees are exceedingly dear to Me. ॥ 20 ॥

**20.s1** — 'अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानाम्' इत्यादावद्वेषादिकं यत् पृथक् प्रीतिमात्रसाधनमुक्तम् 'स मे प्रियः', 'स च मे प्रियः'^२^ इत्यादिना तत् सर्वं मिलितम्, यत्रास्ति **स तु मेऽत्यन्तम्** प्रिय इति प्रागुक्तं पिण्डीकृत्योपसंहरति - येत्विति ।  
→ In "adveṣṭā sarvabhūtānām" etc., whatever was stated — non-hatred and so on — as a separate means merely to [My] pleasure, with "sa me priyaḥ," "sa ca me priyaḥ" etc., all of that is [here] combined: "he in whom [all of it] exists is extremely dear to Me." Bundling together what was stated before, He concludes: "ye tu" etc.

**20.s2** — धर्म्यामृतं धारकत्वाद्धर्मो विष्णुः ।  
→ "Dharmyāmṛtam": dharma is Viṣṇu, because He upholds [everything].

**20.s3** — तदुपासनाङ्गत्वादुपचारेण **धर्म्यम्** ।  
→ Being an aid to His worship, [it is called] "dharmya" by figurative extension.

**20.s4** — अमृतशब्दितमुक्तिसाधनम् **अमृतम्** ।  
→ That which is a means to liberation, [here] called "amṛta" (immortality), is "amṛta."

**20.s5** — धर्म्यं च तदमृतं च **धर्म्यामृतम्** ।  
→ That which is both dharmya and amṛta is "dharmyāmṛta."

**20.s6** — यथोक्तमद्वेष्टेत्यादिनोक्तमद्वेषादिकं प्रीतिसाधनं समस्तम् ।  
→ "As declared" — the whole [set of] non-hatred and the like stated by "adveṣṭā" etc., [which are] means of pleasing [Me].

**20.s7** — **श्रद्दधानाः** आस्तिक्यवन्तः ।  
→ "Śraddadhānāḥ" — possessing faith (āstikya).

**20.s8** — **मत्परमाः** मदुत्तमताज्ञानिनः सन्तः ।  
→ "Matparamāḥ" — being knowers of My supremacy.

**20.s9** — **ये पर्युपासते** सम्यगनुतिष्ठन्ति ।  
→ "Those who faithfully follow" (ye paryupāsate) — practise it properly.

**20.s10** — **ते भक्ताः** ।  
→ "Those devotees."

**20.s11** — **अतीव मे प्रियाः** इत्यर्थः ।  
→ "Exceedingly dear to Me" — this is the meaning. ॥ 20 ॥

**20.s12** — ॥ इति श्रीमद्गीतार्थसङ्ग्रहे श्रीराघवेन्द्रयतिकृते द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥  
→ Thus the Twelfth Chapter, in the gītārthasaṅgraha composed by Śrī Rāghavendra Yati.

**Footnote २** — चित्तद्रवस्तथा स्थैर्यं प्रसादो भक्तिलक्षणम् । आधिक्येन तु तत्रापि स्थैर्यमेव विशेषतः । दम्भस्य चलभक्तेश्च यस्मादश्रादिकं भवेत् । दम्भादिपरिहारार्थं निगृह्णीयाच्च धीरधीः । अत आध्यात्मिकक्लेशैराधिभूताधिदैविकैः । वाक्यैश्च वेदतन्त्राद्यैरुपदेशैश्च तादृशैः । बलवच्छासनैर्वाऽपि यस्य भक्तिर्न चाल्यते । स एव परमो भक्तो विष्णोर्हृदयवल्लभः ॥ - इति भक्तिविवेके ॥ - भा.ता. (११/२४/२४)  
→ "Melting of the heart, steadiness, and serenity are the marks of devotion; and among these, steadiness is especially prominent. Because tears and the like may occur even in the hypocrite and the one of wavering devotion, the wise should restrain [them] to avoid hypocrisy and so on. Therefore, he whose devotion is not shaken by afflictions — ādhyātmika, ādhibhautika and ādhidaivika — nor by statements of the Veda, tantra and the like, nor by such teachings, nor even by forceful commands — he alone is the supreme devotee, the beloved of Viṣṇu's heart." — Bhakti-viveka, as cited in Bhāgavata-tātparya (11/24/24).
